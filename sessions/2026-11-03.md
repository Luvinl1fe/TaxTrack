## The two blockers

**2026–27 WFH fixed rate — still open.** The user pasted a summary saying the rate
"remains 70c", with no source, date or QC number. Searching ato.gov.au, Treasury
and legislation found no published 2026–27 figure. Not acted on: "remains" is the
carry-forward assumption the config exists to refuse. The 🔴 gate stands.

**$1,000 standard deduction — confirmed, and law.** ITAA 1997 s 25-130, inserted
by Schedule 4 of the Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026, No. 49,
Royal Assent 26 June 2026, applying from 2026–27. Read from the Act as made on
legislation.gov.au, not from summaries — which mattered, because the summaries
got the mechanics wrong:

- It **tops up, it doesn't add.** The $1,000 is reduced dollar for dollar by work
  expenses claimed. The pasted summary implied the opposite.
- **Car expenses count** against it (Division 28 is named), unlike the $300 test.
- **Association memberships are disregarded** — union fees are claimed on top.
  Gifts and tax affairs aren't work expenses, so likewise.
- Capped at assessable labour income, which the app doesn't record.

ATO pages still return 403 to automated fetches; Treasury and legislation.gov.au
don't.
